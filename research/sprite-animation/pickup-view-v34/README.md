The rotated-texture matcher now excludes pixels whose bilinear interpolation samples outside the first-frame patch. It retains NCC .95, ambiguity margin .015, support 6px and phase .25 thresholds. The old matcher abstains on two real samples; those results remain in `failure-controls`. The improvement is a narrow rigid-texture observation fix, not general anatomical tracking accuracy.

Visual failures are retained: wrong cape direction/atlas packing in raw outputs, ill-fitting first hair, exposed receiving rims and hollow thigh ends. Actual new hair and filled thigh art replace these failures; thigh-over-shin ordering is explicit. A style/view mismatch or missing part is rejected rather than borrowing another facing.

This is an **agent-reviewed second-view modular motion pilot**. The user has not approved its new art.
