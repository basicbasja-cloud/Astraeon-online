# QA, tools, known failures and limits

## Four separate authorities

1. Native reference pixels/descriptors/timestamps establish observed game presentation.
2. Reconstructed/source rig establishes actual projected joints, owner IDs and authored geometry.
3. Current painted pixels establish visible anatomy, grip, silhouette and style; they can contradict a correct source.
4. User's exact approval receipt establishes acceptance only for named bytes/scope.

Never collapse these into one green score. A passing tool regression proves its tested behavior, not universal perceptual correctness. Current image-owner checks bind manual observations to exact hashes and retained negatives; they do not automatically recover hidden anatomy from arbitrary images.

## What to check for each new bank

* Raw layout/transparency, gutters, no clipping or opaque matte; whole strip provenance and stable shared placement.
* Fixed-view neutral start/return size and head/body socket; all major dimensions and uncertainty, not only overall height.
* Ground support: manually identified heel/toe or visible boot surfaces through all keys; their X/Y travel and stance phase. Largest alpha component's bottom is only a proxy, not a foot detector.
* Source joint projections vs SHA-bound visible painted landmarks; separate residuals, visibility and role identity. Source-only grids cannot certify a generated elbow.
* Anatomical continuity hip→knee→ankle, shoulder→elbow→wrist→grip; near/far and occlusion through crossings; support foot and swinging foot identities.
* Adjacent spatial/velocity changes, meaningful key ordering, holds/events, trajectory amplitude and return pose. Check actual GIF at normal and slow speed, not just contact sheet.
* Runtime layers, hidden surfaces and swap examples; final engine cell origin/clock/events/one-shot stop behavior.

`paint-review.json` v46 only measures actual alpha/component bounds and return differences. It never changes pixels to force a pass. `manual-observations.json` records this turn's visual decisions. Nonzero floor/neutral size discrepancies block claiming a finished grounded animation even if it looks attractive.

## Important negative cases worth keeping

* v41 stable height still hid enlarged rear shoes/arms and held-out neutral mismatch. Global calibration from one key did not fix the art.
* v42 raster width/attachment checks passed while wrist/arm shape remained malformed.
* v43 correct source still produced painted NW key4 collecting with the wrong hand. Renaming masks/relabeling cannot repair wrong pixels.
* v44 static GLB validation passed while animated DQ→LBS surface transfer failed. Actual round-trip frames exposed it; corrective morph proof only covered its sampled frames.
* v45 isolated pass accidentally included animated gem even after render flags. Collection exclusion and non-arm opaque-pixel control fixed it; rejected captures remain.
* High frame count/native generation produced bounce, leg swaps and sliding repeatedly. Never normalize every key or clone stationary shoes to conceal source/art mismatch.

## Integrity scope

All historical evidence manifests and accepted W are checked read-only by `verify-handoff.py`; current native raws and inventory hashes are checked too. Missing historical third-party caches do not become success receipts: see `LOCAL-CACHE-REPORT.json` and retrieval/source documents. Local inventory integrity is separate from rights clearance, model training, commercial asset admission and target-engine validation.

Guide assembly verification note: input atlases were alpha-composited from v45 cells on transparency. The initial raw-RGBA identity assertion failed solely on hidden RGB at alpha=0; retained `artifact-qa-initial-hidden-rgb-FAIL.json` and `guide-composite-diagnosis.json` explain it. The corrected check requires exact known alpha-composite equality for all128 guide cells, and exact raw RGBA identity for all64 painted crops. No visible tolerance/registration was introduced.
