## Findings that change the pipeline

The rejected v22 architecture painted a raised fist, then independently rotated
a prop190° around its palm. The new source study demonstrates the moving wrist,
elbow, shoulder and torso, with the weapon staying in the hand's local frame.
The user prefers the RO representation: **draw the hand sweep, then a separate
tail/smear; no rigid sword needs to be visible during every attack exposure**.
A hidden weapon trajectory can guide the effect without being rendered.

Our four-pose source-conditioned paintover has stronger arm movement, but the
source's lifted rear leg and narrow one-foot base were carried into our artwork.
The user rejects that balance. All three variants (gold blade, silver edit and
body-only) are archived; the full-sword pilot files are superseded unapproved
studies. **No tail was added to conceal the rejected body-only posture.** The
material edit also changes contour pixels; its mask difference is recorded,
and exact pixel preservation is not claimed.

The corrected-phase E generation also fails: half-cycle contact-looking
silhouettes remain too similar and the arm counter-swing is weak. Its raw is
retained in [raw/E-walk-rejected.png](raw/E-walk-rejected.png), not substituted
for the current bank. Using a correct phase12 owner guide did not guarantee
correct generated legs. W masters were never modified.
