# What this actual production attempt taught us

## What worked and what did not

This was not another collection of reference URLs. We imported a fresh external23bone source, evaluated8actual poses, exported deformed-mesh images and named bone heads on a fixed camera, generated original art five times, extracted actual PNG frames, made a clock-bound GIF and measured the resulting image surfaces.

The first2outputs packed cells without hard cut lines but recovery rose too early and the lower authoring row used a different local origin. Attempt3kept lateral foot drift and crossed a cell with its cape. Attempt4improved low recovery through explicit phase/coordinate guidance but crossed two cell boundaries. Attempt5folded the cape inside its slots and preserves a readable whole-body reach. It still fails planted-support and source-relative recovery timing. None of these is an approved actor/part bank.

Repeated boot references and precise prompt coordinates **did not force exact geometry** in these executions. This is evidence about this tool/workflow on this pilot, not a universal impossibility theorem. Additional reading alone cannot make these outputs correct; the factory needs stronger geometry/part constraints and independent visible-image checks.

## Keep joint evidence separate from visible surfaces

Our character's hips, knees and wrists are clothed/armored. The cuff/glove boundary is visible, but it is not an exposed wrist bone center. A boot-toe surface can be tracked, but it is not a hidden ankle coordinate or ground contact. Near/far screen positions are not anatomical left/right.

New image-surface-track.py accepts explicitly defined surface markers, forces anatomical owner unknown and refuses seeds for inferred joints or owner confirmation. It runs the existing image-correspondence method in a scoped adapter without modifying its baseline files. Named joints stay unknown. Actual conservative bidirectional tracking retained0proposals in15held-out marker targets; labelled frames0/3/7remain supplied observations. This does not prove those9seed points are exact, and no detector accuracy percentage is claimed.

New planted-surface-review.py binds images/canvas/exposure clock and manually observed visible points. It measures XandY range, adjacent spacing and time-aware velocity. A static numeric surface check never promotes anatomy/contact/production. Unknowns remain unknown; changed pixels, clocks or phase source are refused. Observations are single-reviewer with3–6pxuncertainty and are provided in a grid for correction.

For this candidate, far-toe55pxX and near-toe62pxX greatly exceed the provisional6pxsupport warning, even though Yvariation is only2/5px. This catches the exact failure that a “same ground height” check would miss. The measurements describe visible toe-cap proxies; they do not recover COM, force, exact sole plane or anatomical side.

## Phase review after source-to-art proportion changes

The source mannequin and painted character have different proportions, so direct raw pixel distance between source bones and character points is not an anatomy score. We compare a declared visible cuff trajectory to source right-wrist projection as a limited phase diagnostic.

Calibrate neutral0and lowreach3for each trajectory; exclude these2points from phase-warning evaluation. Normalize other Ydisplacements by each trajectory's excursion. The source hash, sample time and chosen axis are explicit. This flags frame6with≈0.376fraction disagreement against a provisional0.25warning. It indicates an early rise to review, not a measured wrist localization error or proof of side ownership. Rotation/cloth motion and cuff-vs-bone definition differences remain limitations.

Do not force every body marker to a constant height. Head and hips must lower while bending. Use source-relative expected trajectories for moving parts and fixed-support policies only for surfaces that should stay planted. For attacks, jumps and released feet those policies must change. Do not reuse a pickup support gate blindly for all actions.

## Packing error is different from motion error

The8pose sheet's lower authoring row has roughly32pxorigin difference at neutral endpoints. A single shared row translation corrects that packing origin while preserving scale and internal pose pixels. It is declared and reversible, with foreground clipping checked. No individual frame is fitted to its bbox and no limb is stretched/repositioned numerically.

This repair cannot fix footXmotion or incorrect staging. Keep neutral0/7as packing calibration evidence; do not count matching endpoint support height as independent contact proof. Retain the raw sheets and transform record. Any phase-specific correction requires new art/explicit authored geometry, followed by another actual-image audit.

## Targeted follow-up from actual failures

Six primary documents were retrieved/hash-recorded in targeted-source-receipts.json. Selected documentation sections and three paper abstracts were read; no full-paper replication or model execution is claimed.

| Source | Read finding | Application and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Spine weights](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-weights) | attachment vertices bind to bones; weights deform attachments | author complete parts with explicit geometry/weights; weights need care and cannot repair wrong original art |
| [Spine IK](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-ik-constraints) | bone tips follow targets, keyed bend direction, scale/stretch/softness limitations | foot/grip targets and knee/elbow plane must be explicit; IK does not approve acting/ownership; no paidSpine installation performed |
| [Godot Skeleton2D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_skeleton2d.html) | rest poses and Bone2Dhierarchy, IK modification stack | freeze rest basis/attachment contracts; actual integration not performed here |
| [ControlNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543) | spatial conditioning including pose/depth/segmentation | future bounded geometry-control candidate; paper abstract does not guarantee eight-frame continuity/part replacement |
| [T2I-Adapter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08453) | text alone gives limited granular control; external control signals through adapters | reinforces need for explicit masks/structure rather than stronger prose; no adapter inference/training run |
| [Animate Anyone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17117) | reference appearance, pose guiding and temporal modeling | temporal constraints are separate from identity/detail preservation; no generated video exists in this pilot |

These sources support individual techniques. Their combination into our factory is still a proposed recipe. Public paper abstracts/code names are not permission to download any dataset or a claim the model is integrated.

## Next concrete factory recipe

1. Freeze this external motion source, camera, anatomical names and source time. Keep all rejected own slash poses excluded.
2. Build a complete unarmed character part bank, including hidden content, from approved appearance. Shoes need complete independently replaceable artwork; the cropped boot reference atlas here is conditioning-only, not that bank.
3. For this planted pickup family, fix two shoe parts/anchors in the renderer. Let leg/torso rigging or authored per-pose drawings change above the anchors. Use source knee/elbow planes and calibrated proportions; do not merely stretch the current flat sheet.
4. Use explicit part/pose/depth constraints for appearance redraw, and compare the final image again. A mask or correct source rig is not artistic approval.
5. Check actual visible toe/cuff/head trajectories, full hip→knee→boot ownership where observable, contact/support, cloth depth, endpoint and native-time acting. Preserve unknowns for occluded joints.
6. Pilot hair/garment swaps on the same motion with hidden content/anchors/zorder inspected. Only then expand this exact family to other characters/directions.

Current outcome is a reviewable failing prototype and a stronger error detector, not a complete factory. No current pickup is approved for conditioning new production motions without addressing its measured failures.
