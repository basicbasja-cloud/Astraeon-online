# Lessons and real failure cases

- One connected foreground component does not prove continuous anatomy. Earlier segmented ready arms could remain visually detached while metrics passed. Inspect enlarged full pose, arm/leg owner guide and playback.
- Stable crown and feet do not prove original character proportions. New generated heads often widen; compare canonical original head and neck across actions.
- Exact full-body recomposition is necessary for technical layer extraction but cannot identify wrong owners. Wrongly retained hair in BODY or scarf in HEAD still recomposes exactly. Protect unambiguous hair/body regions and visually trace the neck boundary.
- SAM point satisfaction cannot establish complete head boundaries. Default model contours cut opaque painted fringes. Tight inference context helped one W face and N-dead gold rim, but does not solve all outer hair.
- A forward/reverse pickup can improve bounded motion but is8 drawings/16 exposures. It has no independently authored cloth recovery or item grip. Old v60 still failed feet; do not transfer its general acceptance.
-8 walk drawings repeated over80 exposures remain8 drawings. Two attempts at inbetween generation were not actual halfway poses; count unique source drawings honestly.
- Text “keep planted feet” is unreliable. Use explicit reference/guide and test actual pixels. A row-wide source shift is a rejected generation, not a reason to move every second-row final frame.
- RO is an external direction/pose reference. Community preview clocks are not official game clocks. Source RO body pairing cannot define all head orientation policies.
- A semantic foot ROI must actually include the current view's sole. A copied W window cropped E toes, correctly yielding UNKNOWN. Record corrected ROI and unchanged6px budget; never crop away drift.
- Hidden/merged feet remain UNKNOWN; screen-left/right does not imply anatomical left/right. W pickup's single overlapping visible sole is not two proven planted feet.
- Body/head family calibration is shared bank-wide from neutral. Do not fit crouches to standing silhouette; fixed aspect resize is different from per-pose normalization.
- Ordinary SPA import initially left an empty body cel in the first layer. Reordering empty logical trail/weapon layers first solved construction without changing visible pixels. Preserve native-editable first failure.
- Three concurrent long SPA imports triggered actual E process_timeout. Serial fresh E-native3 passed. A failed process cannot be recorded as an export pass because the source file exists.
- The fast native writer first omitted a destination mkdir. Native-format2 fixes that; independent real SPA PNG export is the evidence, not merely writing a plausible header. Live plan capability schema has no image import; do not invent a hidden fallback.
- Inspect transparent images composited over gray. Hidden RGB can appear as blue/red background in some viewers even withalpha0. Technical mattes preserve source pixels inside support; they are not creative redraw.
- Keep old failures and stale assumptions readable. Status is monotonic evidence, not monotonic success: later hair counterexamples supersede earlier head labels.
