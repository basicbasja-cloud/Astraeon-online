# Production experiments after the fresh foundation

These are proposed experiments, not completed character motions. Dash is allowed as optional research after latest steering; no mechanic is committed. Old own attacks remain excluded.

## Batch A — non-combat baseline

Start with one original character/view and three actions: idle.relaxed, pickup.ground, hit.light. This tests a loop, prop contact and a one-shot/recovery without every failure being interpreted as a sword problem.

For each:
1. choose a complete external reference plus a second targeted reference;
2. inspect all relevant source times, mark phases/contact/uncertainty;
3. author original body-only performance or new rig blocking independently of the excluded old attacks;
4. check native acting, anatomical sides, camera/root/soles and nextstate;
5. redraw our character and repeat actual-pixel checks;
6. trial hair/no-cloak variants before expanding views.

Exit: exact bytes approved per action, event/loop behavior tested; no pending known limb error. File or hash PASS alone cannot satisfy exit.

## Batch B — locomotion transitions

Preserve accepted W pixels. New source studies can propose walk.start/stop/turn and another view, but may not silently overwrite W. Compare in-place versus controller movement to identify sliding. Track paired contacts and half-cycle phase after turns.

Exit: fullspeed smooth, leg alternation reads, no placement bounce, correct cyclic seam; 8views only after a view’s crossing frames pass independent review.

## Batch C — ranged and magic

Test bow draw/hold/release, cast start/loop/release/cancel, hit/block/death. This checks two-hand/string/prop mechanics and state/event timing.

Use noFX first. Add FX after body/weapon review and use one authoritative clock. Test changing attack/cast speed, interrupts, once-only projectile/damage events and terminal states.

Exit: events and palms/targets match, no duplicated release, correctly stopping channel, modular prop bank actually compatible.

## Batch D — fresh melee

From the NEW reference bank choose a single diagonal slash with complete load, execution, follow and recovery. Do not reuse coordinates/painted phases of previous own slash attempts. Include visible or separately modeled sword/hand evidence; a source skeleton without sword cannot certify blade plane or grip.

Observe all relevant intermediate source poses, not just the9overview samples. Make a stable one-view body+weapon performance, review force/intent/lean/stance autonomously, reject weak or arm-collapsed acting before showing a candidate. Then generate our art and check per-pose weapon perspective.

Exit: visually convincing native-speed attack, actual grip/owners/support/trajectories checked, tail phase consistent, complete separate weapon item and correct hand occlusion. No source-only PASS promotion.

## Batch E — reaction and interaction states

Death fall/hold/revive; sit down/hold/up; potion/pickup/talk. Their nextstate, prop visibility and floor/seat contacts establish general factory architecture. Reuse explicit fallback only when compatible with gameplay and visual intent.

Exit: interruption and sequence completion tested; no finite clip forced into a loop, no item double consumption.

## Batch F — production reproduction

Use a second original character with changed proportions/costume and extreme modular items. Keep reference/schema/tools reusable but calibrate camera/rest/contact/palette/budgets anew.

Evaluate held-out facing, overlapping limb pose, long hair, large shield and long weapon. Count rejections/rework/time per action and retain independent annotations. A factory claim requires actual accepted output across multiple actions/items/characters, not the existence of this document.
