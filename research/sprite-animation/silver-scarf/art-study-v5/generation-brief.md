# Generation study brief

The built-in image generation tool received a text-only description, with no reference-image files. This records the intended content and constraints, not a guarantee of repeatable output.

Create one original two-column/two-row four-key fantasy RPG board: ready, load, cut/follow-through, recover. Use a fixed southeast three-quarter view with about 30° elevated orthographic presentation, shared scale and logical ground space. Target compact roughly 96-pixel-tall sprites, enlarged through deliberate clusters and selective colored contours; request 1024×1024 overall. Use a neutral charcoal background and omit labels, scenery and particles.

Keep the user's silver-lavender tousled hair, violet eyes, ochre-gold scarf/cape with star clasp, navy sleeves, ivory split tabard, bronze armor/bracers, brown belt/gloves/boots, olive trousers, muted teal sash, and straight steel sword with gold guard. Aim at approximately 3.3-head proportions. Request restrained 3–5-value material ramps and readable folds, avoiding noisy textures, soft plastic shading and excessive smoothing.

The weapon belongs only to the anatomical right hand, originating at the nearer shoulder on the viewer's left; trace shoulder/elbow/wrist/glove/grip even through a cross-body cut. The other hand remains empty. Load must sink and shift hips onto the rear leg, bend the rear knee and coil the chest with the sword raised outside the hair silhouette. Cut must drive hips/chest, absorb on the lead knee, lift the rear heel while keeping toe contact and pull the empty arm back. Recovery recenters hips/chest and settles cloth after body movement. Body motion should read without a blade effect; do not clone the legs.

Actual delivered canvas, source hash and limits are recorded in [provenance](study-provenance.json). The generated board does not supply the requested source registration, native pixel grid or complete parts; see [visual inspection](visual-review.json). It stays a direction study.
