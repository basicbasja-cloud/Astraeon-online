## Experiments tied to the sources

The first source had poor visibility of the picking hand; the next improved reach but had an unsuitable lift route/cape interaction. Those renders remain in`source-attempt1`and`source-attempt2`. The primary current source adds a genuine ground target and distinct grip socket. The source before the ground/socket fix has an archived JSON and exact native input copies; it is not silently rewritten into a successful contact proof.

Fourteen source checks pass, including actual full-scene contact visibility and independent projection. The procedural rig is an **original study**, with CC0PickUp timing inspiration from the prior study, rather than an unchanged preset. Animated bones can be reopened, but the saved geometry is a terminal procedural snapshot; all actual per-key mesh captures are separately retained.

Eight retained native still outputs comprise seven full-strip redraws and one deepest-key repair guide. The latter improves two-hand occlusion; later whole strips still fail image-space registration. Native6's actual12/23pxhorizontal contact drift and native7's17/32pxfailures show that valid source goals and owner guides do not constrain an unconstrained repaint enough. Six source/painted input guides reproduce byte-for-byte; no guide patches become final art.

The approved v39 GIF is the empirical motion/clock reference. Its exact bytes, decoded images and durations are actually used by the checker and viewer. It does not license new-view geometry or turn deeper target interaction into an already accepted motion. This separation is the main source-of-truth rule for the next character and next action too.
