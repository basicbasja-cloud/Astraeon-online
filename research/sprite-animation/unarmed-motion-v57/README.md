# Original in-place unarmed motion

Four ready loops (24 evaluated exposures/1070ms) and four right-hand punch clips (16 exposures/1170ms) use fresh CC0 KayKit `Melee_Unarmed_Idle` and `Melee_Unarmed_Attack_Punch_A` references. They do not reuse old project slash animations. General `Idle_A` supplies semantic neutral calibration. Exact source GLB hashes, creator/license and257 actual samples per external clip are in `source/cc0-motion.json`.

The external punch steps forward. This case deliberately authors an **in-place adaptation**: original neutral feet and planar pelvis stay fixed, original bone lengths are solved with leg IK, external upper-body directions/timing and relative pelvis-height motion are retained. A single6cm whole-clip pelvis clearance is computed from source-only reachability with5mm knee margin, before any raster export. It is not a per-frame image correction or exact replay of the external lunge.

Both saved Blender actions have fake users. `export-source-endpoints.py` reopens the saved `.blend`, verifies both actions and independently evaluates1025 samples/action. Missing inactive actions were caught and retained as a negative. Always run Blender with `--python-exit-code 1`: a Python traceback otherwise may still return shell success.

Original painted parts remain fixed in rest space. Complete existing native N arm modules replace walking-only partially occluded forearms. S has static source-ownership corrections for neighboring cape/belt pixels, trouser/native kneeguard overlap, and bounded existing native hidden-cape underlap. `owned-parts-ledger.json` records original input hashes, masks, bank scales and constant rest origins. No frame fitting, shoe pasting or pose-dependent asset scaling is used.

`SpriteOwnedSegmentChain` gives armor one UV owner, permits a declared dark-sleeve underlap, with each armor feature assigned to its declared part. A torso-fixed pauldron experiment exposed sleeve gaps and was rejected. N native wrist313 and fist329 are explicitly rebound to the actual metal/glove boundary; keeping the walking wrist323 cut through the fist and was rejected. Rounded sleeve underlap avoids square elbow tabs. Material masks are manually bounded for these source assets, not automatic anatomical segmentation. Legs retain a continuous fixed binding selected by geometry-only search; E-left hidden hip/knee anchors are calibrated from actual neutral source offsets around the art ankle. Current images, not the search score alone, require review.

Two retargeting mistakes were caught in actual images even though triangle areas were positive. The original head bone points backward; interpreting its projected head-to-tail line as vertical flipped the rear head. The driver now projects canonical world-up after the actual saved head rotation. Original/source neutral arm directions also differ: preserving those angle biases produced a low/misdirected punch. The current retarget preserves actual external wrist position relative to its shoulder with a single whole-arm length gain, solves original-length elbows in the source elbow plane, and keeps a closed-fist wrist aligned to its forearm. Final2D directions match the saved adapted source, without a neutral-angle bias.

`check-fight.py` validates current hashes, source geometry, every sampled arm direction, actual separate head/body GPU overlap, visible lower-shoe strips and frame endpoints. Legs must keep area ratio>.15. An arm submesh must match its single analytic bone affine ratio at every triangle; rigid wrist/fist parts retain ratio1. The N ready counter forearm projects nearly edge-on (minimum~.08529); it is not a local fold. That mathematical fact does not establish painted anatomy. Initial head flips, partial forearms, armor duplication and continuous-leg folds remain rejected; the original rejected image set is in `negative-initial`.

`fight-review.html` is an authoring viewer; the final common-canvas PNG viewer and all-action acceptance record live in [v56](../core-family-v56/README.md). This case does not establish physical impacts, game hit timing, hidden anatomy, modular swaps or user acceptance. Native jobs were reused from earlier new cases; there are no new paid external calls. Original host-image billing remains unknown.
