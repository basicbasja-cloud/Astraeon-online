# RO1 placement and hard RO3 scale review

The user rejected the 4–5-unit neighborhood streets as too narrow. All ten
supplied RO3 screenshots were re-inspected on 6 October 2026. The supplied RO1
Prontera map and [the official Prontera guide](https://ragnarokonline.gungho.jp/gameguide/worldmap/prontera.html)
inform city-block placement principles: a clear central public space, a royal
precinct, substantial inhabited blocks, broad connecting streets, owned planted
plots and a connected perimeter route. Wayfarer retains its own geometric plan;
Prontera's coordinates, terrain and proprietary models are not imported.

The rejected draft confused close building gaps with narrow public streets.
Reducing every connecting road to 4–5 units created an alley network. The revised
hierarchy uses 9-unit district streets, a 10-unit perimeter circuit and 8-unit
cross streets. The ceremonial axis/cross remain 14/12. Four redundant parallel
through lanes and an unused parterre lane are removed, so wider streets bound
larger inhabited blocks with shared back courts. Close side gaps remain between
buildings. All 172 existing candidate houses are kept as complete models and
reorganized; actor art and model scale are not adjusted to disguise the street.

These are Astraeon world units, not measurements recovered in metres from an
RO3 screenshot. Screenshot scale depends on camera pitch, yaw, perspective and
the nearby person. The unchanged Warrior registration corresponds to roughly
2.4 world units of painted height. At the existing 46-degree comparison pitch,
an 8–9-unit street viewed across its width is approximately 4.8–5.4 painted
person-heights in the local projection; a 4.5-unit street is about 2.7. This is a
projection check, not a claim of exact reconstruction of RO3 geometry. The final
rendered comparison and ordinary walking must confirm the resulting scale.

The current variation includes complete original service/house models, smaller
townhouses and larger two-storey frontage models. Review their doors, storeys,
roof mass, side wings, fountain and civic hierarchy against nearby people. A
house count or equal map dimensions does not establish a scale match.

RO1 Prontera's known 312 × 392 navigation-cell dimensions are recorded separately
in `research/prontera-scale-reference.json`; those cells are not interchangeable
with Astraeon world units. Blueprint placement is an example, while all ten RO3
images remain the hard quality and person-relative scale target.

## Owned neighborhood courts

The latest steering adds two inward-facing owned gathering areas within the
formal street-defined blocks. Seven whole models face shared features after the square-frontage revision:
three homes/shops at Artisans’ Court and four frontages at Willow Court. Wells,
benches, planting, craft tables and a noticeboard provide a local purpose. Side
passages connect to the main streets. These courts complement the public street
hierarchy; they do not reduce its reviewed widths. Two existing local residents
walk court loops, with identities, artwork, pace and actor budgets preserved.

## The fountain square as a gathering center

Ten nearby complete building frontages now face the square: four along its
north approach, two on either flank and two on its south edge. Entrances,
awnings and accessories rotate with the models. Three neighboring modules
shift slightly to preserve roof/awning gaps; the fountain, ceremonial widths
and both owned courts remain. Two edge models join the square frontage, leaving
three inward-facing models at Artisans’ Court and four at Willow Court. An
existing local resident walks the square perimeter. Fresh rendered views and
ordinary input at the unchanged default camera verify this revision.
