# Swordsman Pickup SW — first nine painted poses, rejected

A 3×3 image-generation study painted the first nine poses of the 35-sample SW Pickup guide over a fixed, identical crop. It uses the preserved same-direction Swordsman identity. The nine poses were requested as complete clothed bodies in one sheet, not assembled limbs. The model and seed were not exposed. [Guide versus draft GIF](first-nine-comparison.gif); raw guide contact sheet, prompt and generated sheet are in `authoring/characters/factory-v3/patch-0.0.1/painted-candidates/swordsman-male-pickup-sw-000-008-20261029/`.

**Rejected as a master:** the output is larger than the guide and changes size/placement differently as the character kneels. After one uniform whole-sheet resize to 1152×1152, frame 0 dark-foreground bounding boxes in a 384px cell are guide `[133,81,249,320]` and draft `[106,47,273,362]`; frame 8 is guide `[104,206,275,334]` and draft `[72,126,336,315]`. These boxes include garments and sword, not anatomical measurements. The mismatch is large enough to require guide-relative landmark and body correction, not per-frame bbox fitting. The sword, limbs, contact and anatomy were not approved.

The preview has nine of 35 poses, uses 100 ms/frame with a final pause, and repeats for inspection. It is not game timing or a complete loop. The white background has not been extracted or labeled; no cutouts, head/body/weapon variants or runtime binding were produced. Do not scale and admit this sheet to claim a completed action. Future work must establish a pose-accurate painting method, then verify master continuity, same-frame cutouts, and gameplay.
