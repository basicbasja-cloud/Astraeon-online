# Mage frame005 foot painting audit — not accepted

Two separate own-frame, bounded image-paint attempts were made for the shoes. The second used the frame's evaluated source ankle positions plus a frame0 painted sole-offset proposal as guide marks. Neither local painting improved the measured horizontal sole positions in the simple raster diagnostic. The vertical-shift options create questionable seams and are not accepted. No original master or ankle/shin pixels outside the mask changed, and no limb or frame was copied.

[Source ankle comparison, frames0/5/11/16](source-ankles.jpg) · [unmarked guide attempt](plain-comparison.jpg) · [marked contact attempt](annotated-comparison.jpg)

For a consistent local comparison at alpha threshold128, original frame005 sole medians were approximately (337,561) and (439,557); the unshifted generated options measured (336,561)/(439,557) and (336,561)/(440,557). The guide-relative proposed targets were approximately (341,558)/(435,559). Those medians and targets are **not verified anatomical contact landmarks**: boot toe shape can shift the value. Source projected ankle dots remain close to the painted ankle. Do not move an entire leg, use another frame's boot or claim the gait fixed based on these numbers.

Raw images, prompts, inputs, masks, generation metadata and bounded-patch producers are retained. Further work needs correctly reviewed foot/contact landmarks and actual temporal/transition review. This one rejected study adds no runtime coverage; the goal remains active.
