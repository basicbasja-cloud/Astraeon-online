# Source-first paintover and visible recovery ownership

Executed case: `research/sprite-animation/painted-pickup-v47/`. This is a bounded improvement, not a production recipe or accepted all-view bank.

Putting a known-wrong painted bank into image conditioning, even explicitly marked negative, reproduced its wrong-hand lift in v47attempt1. Whole-bank source-first conditioning without that bank gave much more stable actual boot pixels in attempt2. A targeted native edit of attempt2 then made the right glove/item visible in recovery without manually cloning shoes or per-key alignment. This single comparison motivates the method; it does not establish general model causality or determinism. All attempts stay retained.

1. Start with exact current pose source, anatomical owner guide and separately scoped accepted identity. Failed art is a review negative, not a positive generation example.
2. Check generated pixels independently. Source correctness and prompt handedness do not certify a grip. Bind manual observations to the raw SHA, name camera/view and visible shoulder/forearm/glove connection; never infer anatomy solely from screenX or item color.
3. Treat covered contact as UNKNOWN. A visible recovery hold proves that exposure only; it does not recover hidden grasp joints or certify its whole trajectory.
4. Use fixed image-surface windows for surface measurements. If a window touches another garment, abstain. A revised distal-only window may support a narrower surface claim, with old failures retained, unchanged thresholds and explicit excluded anatomy. Do not call alpha-component bottoms sole tracking.
5. Reinspect after targeted edits: imagegen is not guaranteed to preserve neighboring pixels. New crop hashes, surface measures, neutral/return dimensions, ownership and clocks all need rechecking.
6. Keep strict production gates blocked for missing family dimensions, contacts, slots/swaps and runtime tests even when a specific surface/ownership defect improves. Current407/404px neutral/return is not the accepted399px different-view family contract.
