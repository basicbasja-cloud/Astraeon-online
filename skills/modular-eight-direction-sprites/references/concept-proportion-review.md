# Compare actual concept proportions before locking a sprite family

[Executed v37 review](../../../research/sprite-animation/proportion-audit-v37/README.md), [bound measurement report](../../../research/sprite-animation/proportion-audit-v37/proportion-report.json).

Correct neck attachment is independent of correct proportions. Compare to the original concept, not an intermediate redraw whose proportions may already have drifted. If the concept is only visible in the conversation and its original attachment bytes are missing, say so; keep qualitative review separate from candidate numeric measurements. Never assign invented source hashes, automatic landmarks or a precise similarity score to a missing original. Do not promote a derived reference to the concept source of truth.

For static review, normalize each **complete** character uniformly to the same crown-to-sole height. Exclude weapon/trail/shadow bounds from character-height measurements, and verify the visible extents. This normalization is not a per-key runtime registration fix. Keep the original images intact. A full head layer can include neck underlap; use the visible jaw separately when measuring crown-to-jaw height. In v36neutral the full head layer is116pxhigh, but crown-to-visible-jaw is105px in a408pxfigure, about25.7%. Do not confuse those ratios.

Measure projected hairstyle width, armor span, belt level and boot proportions independently. Manual visible armor edges are not detected anatomical shoulder joints. Facing angle and camera pitch change apparent shoulder breadth and leg length. Do not score a profile against a front-quarter reference as a matched-camera anatomical error. Do not mirror asymmetrical items, squash a head horizontally or stretch a torso to force unmatched views into agreement. Author a physical neutral at the intended reference camera first; then redraw the actual silhouette if required.

V37finds near-quarter-height head but broad-looking hair relative to the current projected armor. Head129px/armor101px is a current-image ratio, not a concept-error percentage. A blanket smaller-head fix is not justified by height alone. The review remains NOT_LOCKED and introduces no universal numeric acceptance threshold. Known motion/contact failures and all prior evidence remain unchanged; static review grants no new user-art, motion, all-view or factory approval.
