# Binding user constraints

Recorded 2026-10-11 during research iteration 2. These override earlier inferred project references.

1. Continue research and study until there is practical confidence in producing the game's sprites.
2. The game is heavily inspired by Ragnarok.
3. The main branch is dead; use the newest branch.
4. All character assets in every branch are rejected. Do not study them or use them as style, anatomy, pose, palette, silhouette, or production references.
5. Clarification: the general art style is acceptable; animation quality is the failure. Preserve the broad visual direction, prioritize animation mechanics and coherence, and keep rejected branch assets excluded as study inputs. This does not approve any particular sheet or animation.
6. Character animation should run at higher FPS than classic Ragnarok. Treat the classic game's timing and low-frame examples as historical references, not the new production ceiling. Compare higher pose rates while preserving contact, weight, identity, and deliberate attack timing.

Implementation code, game contracts, and non-art requirements can be inspected on the newest branch. External primary references and fresh isolated experiments are the permitted study basis. Never treat the presence of an asset in a branch as approval.

Before the fourth constraint arrived, the old checkout's warrior sheet and scene screenshot were inspected. The resulting art/style inferences are superseded and must not inform new asset production. Existing research entries that describe those files are historical observations only.

The general art direction is acceptable to the user. No individual production seed, animation, or newly generated character has been approved. A successful technical prototype cannot by itself establish animation or artistic acceptance.
