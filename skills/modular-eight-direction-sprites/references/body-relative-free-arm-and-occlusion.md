# Body-relative free-arm motion and occlusion checks

User rejects the left-arm bend in some v44 views. [v45 evidence](../../../research/sprite-animation/left-arm-repair-v45/README.md), [full know-how](../../../research/sprite-animation/left-arm-repair-v45/KNOW-HOW.md).

1. Separate target compliance, shape preservation and intended acting. The real old rig retains bone lengths and skin sections but fails the new relaxed-pickup arm-plane contract. Do not let source/area passes override user-visible anatomy/readability failures.
2. Author a free arm relative to the torso, including its hand target and elbow pole. Strong outward world-space poles can turn a natural-looking view into a sideways fold in another. Use the actual shoulder-line/up/forward basis; the current no-yaw shortcut is not a general turning-actor adapter.
3. Reopen the saved rig and measure actual bone vectors at intermediate times. Check upperarm lateral component, elbow-plane normal, signed normal continuity, flexion and fixed lengths. Current20°/25°guards apply to this relaxed free-arm pickup only. They are not universal anatomical limits or a painted-image detector.
4. Inspect evaluated skin vertices as well: binding, enabled modifiers, normalized nonnegative weights, closed surface, cross-section and longitudinal ring spacing. Area alone misses bad acting; none of these is a complete collision/anatomy proof.
5. Preserve the same physical camera/phase and prior display transform for before/after. An unchanged source bank does not require shaded RGB equality when sampling noise or shadows change. No per-frame/view fit, manual limb relabeling or diagnostic recoloring as a repair.
6. Render actual depth-tested arm surfaces and separate disclosed isolated references. A hidden arm stays hidden in the full actor. Low visible fraction means image-side UNKNOWN. Verify isolated outputs contain only the target surface: animated object visibility can undo a temporary hide; collection-level exclusion is robust for this diagnostic.
7. Keep real old failure files as negative controls. Renaming the old report must not pass. Retain fixed timing, head/body scale and unaffected right-hand contact/feet. Distinguish exact-key precision from between-bake subpixel residuals.
8. Approval does not transfer: current rig is a blockout; final art, arbitrary poses, anatomy in occluded views, head/body sprite replacement and game import remain unapproved. The older v44 GLB receipts do not validate the new left-arm action. Use free local methods, preserve frozen evidence/W and the sole research branch.
