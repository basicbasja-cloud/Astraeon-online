Original eight-direction motion study

Diagnostic geometry for grounding, shared pose timing, and interchangeable parts. This is a fresh engineering exercise, not final character art or an approved animation. Blue boot = anatomical left; orange boot = anatomical right.

All views sample one pose. Green rings indicate planted feet. The moving grid shows root-relative ground travel; a planted foot stays aligned with a ground line during its contact interval. Swapping parts preserves the clock. Attack hit landmark is a diagnostic phase, not a gameplay damage implementation.

Run: python -m http.server 8765 --directory studies/motion-lab from the skill package. Tests: node studies/motion-lab/motion.test.mjs. Source and scope: README.
