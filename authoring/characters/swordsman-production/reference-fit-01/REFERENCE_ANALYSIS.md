# Reference-fit checkpoint 01

10 October 2026. Parent concept remains candidate-01, SHA256
`2fa0145d82ae3eabd6c04aadbf89d485b2bf0b0db3c228d278ca95b7f463253b`.

Purpose: decide body/head proportions and relaxed standing pose before detailed
sculpting, fitted skeleton, costumes, painted finish or animation. New geometry
is built from this reference; no rejected mesh, rig or turnaround is imported.

The approved RGBA image is 1254 square. Pixels with alpha greater than 128 span
x 269..893, y 91..1203. The full silhouette is normalized uniformly to 176 px
for the initial comparison, never separately stretching body, arms or head.
The ground/root guide is estimated at (645,1155) in reference pixels; it is
mapped to (160,264). The two soles sit at different projected heights because
the feet have depth separation in the elevated view. They are not forced onto
one horizontal image line.

The character faces screen-right in a three-quarter elevated view. The initial
authoring hypothesis is a fixed orthographic camera at 25 degrees elevation and
SE character heading +45 degrees. Camera projection is a fitting hypothesis,
not information recoverable uniquely from one painted image.

Primary masses: broad tousled hair above a compact skull; short chest/waist;
ivory split tunic and dark trousers; substantial boots and bracers; mustard
scarf/cape; sword in the right hand, down and forward across the nearer leg.
The near right arm drops with a soft elbow bend. The far left forearm and fist
hang outside the torso. Knees are slightly flexed; the near foot carries more
visual weight while the far foot is offset back. These are visible-pose targets,
not a new animation or anatomical acceptance.

Initial manual landmarks have uncertainty of approximately 10-20 reference
pixels. They describe visible mass/joint centers; clothing obscures shoulder,
hip and knee joints. Depth and the underlying anatomy are inferred. The web
review retains the unchanged reference with a toggleable landmark guide and a
common camera/scale comparison. Landmark projection agreement is bookkeeping,
not independent evidence of likeness.

Palette for broad form separation only: silver/lavender hair, warm neutral face,
mustard cape, bronze armor, ivory tunic, navy underclothes, olive trousers,
teal hip cloth and brown boots/gloves. Light from upper/front left with fill.
No painted identity, facial finish, emblem or material-detail approval is sought.

The first source is a continuous low-detail volume blockout and removable major
clothing volumes. It is not final deformation topology and is not skinned.
Only after proportion/pose acceptance should retopology and fitted Rigify
controls establish reusable motion. Sword blade position and hand mass are
provisional; palm/thumb/finger contact is the subsequent visual gate. Endpoint
fit alone cannot approve the grasp.
