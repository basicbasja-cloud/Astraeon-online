# Swordsman V3 independent art concepts

**Provider:** OpenAI ImageGen
**Reference images:** the two high-detail Swordsman images supplied in the conversation. The hair prompt also used the detached hair concept generated earlier in this same pilot.
**Purpose:** independent component art concepts, not runtime-ready animation layers.

Each request used a transparent background. The images are stored separately in `../raw/` and tracked with hashes in `../manifest.json`.

## Hair

Create one production-quality modular sprite asset, not a character portrait: ONLY the silver/lavender-white tousled fantasy swordsman's hair, viewed from the same elevated three-quarter game camera as the two supplied reference images. Preserve the distinct silhouette of many clean layered locks, the swept fringe shape, light silver strands, lavender shadows, crisp precise illustrated edges, and polished 2D fantasy RPG painting from the references. Hair only: omit face, head, ears, neck, body, armor, cloak, sword, highlights outside the hair silhouette, and all text. Show the complete hair mass from crown through bangs on a transparent background, centered and large, with no shadow or glow. Do not trace the reference; redraw as original Astraeon art. High detail, sharp contour and controlled small highlights, no blur, haze, noisy texture, soft focus, 3D render, background, box, or sheet grid.

Create one separate transparent 2D game-art layer for Astraeon's silver-haired fantasy swordsman: the complete underlying male BaseBody only, full standing neutral three-quarter pose matching the two supplied references' elevated game camera and proportions. No armor, shoulder plates, belts, gloves, decorative cloth, sword, cloak, cape, FX, or background. Right hand posed around an invisible sword grip. It must work as the replaceable body under independent clothing, hair, weapon, and cloak assets; draw complete body surfaces beneath garments.
