Astraeon Factory V3 — Golden Swordsman master identity and motion prompt

Create the same original male fantasy Swordsman in every action, direction, and frame. Use the approved V2 crop as identity authority and the V3 structural render only as pose, perspective, timing, and root guidance. Do not copy another game's sprite design.

IDENTITY
- Youthful storybook warrior with short tousled silver/lavender-grey hair and a broad swept fringe that partly hides the eyes.
- Bronze and mustard-gold armor over a split ivory tunic, dark olive/navy underlayers, teal waist detail, brown leather belts and gloves, and tall dark boots.
- A straight, narrow, one-handed steel sword with a warm bronze/gold hilt, held in the anatomical right hand.
- No trailing cape by default. Keep BackAccessory separate and optional; toggling it must not alter the body, pose, or framing.
- Preserve the V2 elevated fantasy-sprite camera, body proportions, and stable root. Use eight real unmirrored directions in this order: S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE.

ART DIRECTION
- High-quality, crisp 2D fantasy RPG game illustration. Use precise small-scale edges, clear armor seams, defined facial planes, polished controlled highlights, and restrained two-tone warm shading. Keep forms clean and readable at 160px runtime-cell size.
- Keep the palette centered on silver-lavender, mustard-gold, bronze, ivory, dark olive/navy, teal, and dark brown.
- Avoid blur, airbrush haze, fuzzy/colored edge pixels, noisy texture, soft focus, photorealism, a 3D-render appearance, hard black outlines, plastic shine, modern tactical details, background effects, and unrelated costume or weapon changes.

ANIMATION QUALITY
- Every action should read as a real human body carrying a sword: believable balance, torso mechanics, foot contacts, and continuous weight transfer.
- Idle: sword-ready stance with subtle breathing and a small believable weight shift.
- Walk: natural alternating footfalls, steady torso, and a relaxed sword arm; avoid exaggerated marching or a sliding gait.
- BasicAttack: deliberate, energetic fantasy low sweep with anticipation, hip and torso weight transfer, clear contact, controlled follow-through, and recovery back to guard. Avoid deep forward bows, flailing, stiff arm-only movement, and toy-like swings.
- Preserve the actual source motion and frame order. Do not invent new poses to fill the sheet. Do not mirror directions.

SPRITE SHEET CONTRACT
- For an 8x8 guide-based sheet, use directions as columns and chronological frames as rows, with no labels, cell borders, or scene background.
- Keep one character scale across the complete action set, preserve the guide's in-place pose offsets, and align the feet/root to the established 160x160 runtime cell and (80,132) ground anchor.
- Keep every character and weapon inside its cell, keep all 64 cells visually consistent, and use transparent pixels outside the sprite.
- Prefer one coherent character painting across the sheet, then derive and repair modular layers from structural guides. Never claim a fused composite is independently modular.
